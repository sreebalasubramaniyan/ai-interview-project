AI CODING INTERVIEW APPLICATION

this is an app for conducting a coding interview in online, so these are the main features of our app

Feature

1. Admin
    Admin Can create an interview by setting the questions either by manually or just give the leetcode url
    then our app automatically scraps that leetcode question and (either generate the testcases or take them by some other resources) he can set up the interview for a particular interviewee by the email id
    once he set the meeting with time and date, the email will be sent to the interviwee

2. Interviee
    By clicking that email user will redirected to the interview portal where he can see the coding question
    and he can choose the language and run and submit the code(just like leetcode) by the certain amount of time 
    once the interview is over 
    the report/result will be sent to the admin's dashboard
